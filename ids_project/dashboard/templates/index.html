Alert Notification Configuration Wizard

Configure outbound incident alerting channels. NetGuard dispatches qualifying alerts asynchronously via background outbox workers with cooldown deduplication.

How Email Works in NetGuard: NetGuard does not run its own mail server. It connects securely to your SMTP relay (e.g. Gmail, Office 365, or internal Postfix) using your credentials, and sends alerts from your Sender Address to your Recipient Address.
Quick Presets:
Telegram Bot Setup: 1. Create a bot via @BotFather to get your Bot Token. 2. Message @userinfobot or add your bot to a channel to get your Chat ID.
Webhook Delivery: Sends HTTP POST JSON alert payloads to your SIEM, SOAR, or custom endpoint. Includes HMAC-SHA256 signature header X-NetGuard-Signature when secret is specified.
Twilio SMS Gateway: Dispatches high/critical incident SMS alerts via Twilio REST API. Requires active Twilio Account SID, Auth Token, and verified phone number. Standard carrier SMS rates apply.
